Job Title: Data Scientist

Overview
InsightCart (a fictional retail analytics company) is hiring a data scientist to build customer-churn and demand-forecasting models.

Responsibilities
- Build, validate and deploy machine learning models using Python and scikit-learn
- Analyse large datasets with SQL and Pandas and communicate insights to business teams
- Develop NLP pipelines to classify customer reviews
- Present results with clear data visualisation

Qualifications
- 2-4 years of experience in data science or machine learning roles
- Strong Python, SQL, statistics and Pandas skills
- Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch for deep learning
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science or a related field; a Master's degree is preferred

Good to Have
- Familiarity with Tableau or Power BI
- Experience deploying models with Docker; knowledge of Spark is a plus
